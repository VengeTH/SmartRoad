# SmartRoad — Data Retention Policy

> **Last updated:** 2026-06-09  
> **A product of The Heedful**

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how long SmartRoad retains user data and the procedures for data deletion.

## 2. Data Categories & Retention Periods

| Category | Examples | Retention | Rationale |
|----------|----------|-----------|-----------|
| **Account Data** | Name, email, profile photo | Until account deletion | Core service delivery |
| **Authentication Logs** | Login timestamps, IP addresses | 12 months | Security monitoring |
| **Location Data** | GPS during navigation | 30 days | Service improvement |
| **Route Data (yours)** | Routes you created | Until you delete them | Your content, your control |
| **Route Data (purchased)** | Routes you bought | License duration | Marketplace integrity |
| **Hazard Reports** | Pothole, flood, etc. | Until resolved + 2 years | Community reference |
| **Comments & Ratings** | Reviews, feedback | Until you delete them | Community continuity |
| **Sticker Data** | Uploaded sticker images | Until sticker expires | HOA access verification |
| **Gate Access Logs** | Guard scan records | 3 years | Security audit trail |
| **Fuel Price Updates** | Price submissions | 2 years | Historical pricing |
| **Analytics** | Usage patterns, crashes | 6 months | App improvement |
| **Communications** | Support emails | 1 year | Customer service |
| **Backup Data** | All of the above | 90 days (separate from primary) | Disaster recovery |

## 3. Data Deletion

### 3.1 User-Initiated Deletion
- You can delete individual routes, comments, and reports at any time
- Account deletion can be requested via the Profile screen or by emailing **gdpr@smartroad.app**
- Upon account deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days

### 3.2 Automatic Deletion
- Expired data is automatically purged daily
- Stale session tokens are invalidated after 7 days of inactivity

### 3.3 Retention for Legal Purposes
Data may be retained longer if required by:
- Applicable laws (e.g., tax records, anti-fraud)
- Ongoing legal proceedings
- Law enforcement requests

## 4. Anonymization

Where complete deletion is not possible (e.g., aggregated analytics), data will be anonymized so it cannot be linked to you.

## 5. Backup Data

Data in backups is retained for a maximum of 90 days. Restoring from backup will restore data as it existed at the time of backup.

## 6. Third-Party Data

Data shared with third-party services (Supabase, OpenStreetMap, etc.) is subject to their respective retention policies.

## 7. Contact

For data deletion requests: **gdpr@smartroad.app**

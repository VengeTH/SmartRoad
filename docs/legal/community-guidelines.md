# SmartRoad — Community Guidelines

> **Last updated:** 2026-06-09  
> **A product of The Heedful**

---

## Our Mission

SmartRoad's community features exist to make Philippine roads safer and more navigable for everyone. These guidelines help maintain a helpful, respectful, and trustworthy community.

## 1. Be Accurate

- Report hazards only if you have personally seen them
- Provide accurate descriptions, locations, and severity levels
- Do not submit false or exaggerated reports
- Update or remove reports when hazards are resolved

## 2. Be Respectful

- Treat other users with courtesy
- No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks
- No spam, advertising, or self-promotion
- Keep comments relevant to the road or location

## 3. Be Honest

- Do not create fake accounts
- Do not artificially inflate ratings or reviews
- Do not manipulate reputation or trust scores
- Route sellers: accurately describe your routes

## 4. Be Safe

- Never use the App while driving
- Pull over before reporting hazards or using features
- Do not share personal contact information publicly
- Report unsafe content to moderators

## 5. Content Standards

### ✅ Allowed
- Hazard reports with clear descriptions
- Route reviews with constructive feedback
- Photos of road conditions, fuel prices, or stickers
- Helpful comments about entrances and shortcuts

### ❌ Not Allowed
- Offensive, obscene, or illegal content
- Copyrighted material you don't own
- Personal information (phone numbers, addresses)
- Misleading or fraudulent content
- Commercial advertising

## 6. Reputation & Trust

- Build reputation through accurate reports and helpful contributions
- Verified reporters (Expert level) have their reports weighted higher
- Abusing the reputation system may result in suspension
- Reporter levels: Novice → Verified → Expert → Admin

## 7. Reporting Violations

To report content that violates these guidelines:
1. Use the "Report" button on the content
2. Contact moderators via the App
3. Email: **community@smartroad.app**

## 8. Enforcement

| Violation | First Offense | Repeated |
|-----------|---------------|----------|
| Inaccurate report | Warning | Report removal + reputation penalty |
| Disrespectful comment | Removal | 7-day suspension |
| Spam | Warning | Account suspension |
| Fraudulent content | Account suspension | Permanent ban |
| Illegal content | Immediate removal | Permanent ban + legal action |

## 9. Appeals

If you believe enforcement was incorrect, appeal to **appeals@smartroad.app**.

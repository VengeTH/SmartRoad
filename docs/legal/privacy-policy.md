## 1. Introduction

SmartRoad ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Name, email address, and profile photo when you register.
- **Route Data**: Routes you create, save, share, or purchase through the marketplace.
- **Reports**: Hazard reports, comments, ratings, and other community contributions.
- **Communications**: Messages you send to us or through our platform.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Location Data**: Real-time GPS location when using navigation features. Location data is collected only while the app is in use or with your explicit permission.
- **Device Information**: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers.
- **Usage Data**: Features used, pages viewed, time spent in the app.
- **Crash Data**: Error logs and crash reports to improve app stability.

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- **Authentication Providers**: When you sign in using third-party services.
- **Map Data Providers**: OpenStreetMap, GraphHopper, and other mapping services.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

| Purpose | Legal Basis |
|---------|-------------|
| Provide navigation and routing services | Contractual necessity |
| Process route marketplace transactions | Contractual necessity |
| Display community hazard reports | Legitimate interest |
| Send emergency alerts and notifications | Consent / Vital interest |
| Improve app performance and features | Legitimate interest |
| Comply with legal obligations | Legal obligation |

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

- **Service Providers**: Cloud infrastructure (Supabase, Docker), mapping services, push notification providers.
- **HOAs / Villages**: Sticker verification status (only with your consent).
- **Legal Authorities**: When required by law or to protect rights and safety.
- **Community**: Your public profile, routes, and hazard reports are visible to other users.

## 5. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|------------------|
| Account information | Until account deletion |
| Route data (your own) | Until you delete it |
| Route data (purchased) | Duration of license |
| Hazard reports | Until resolved or 2 years |
| Location history | 30 days |
| Communications | 1 year |
| Analytics / logs | 6 months |

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and data
- Restrict or object to processing
- Data portability
- Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at **privacy@smartroad.app**.

## 7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures including:
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Encryption at rest
- Row-level security on databases
- Regular security audits
- Access controls and authentication

## 8. Location Data

Location data is critical to our service. You can control location permissions through your device settings. Note that disabling location may limit app functionality.

## 9. Children's Privacy

SmartRoad is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed on servers located in various countries. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for cross-border data transfers.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes through the app or email.

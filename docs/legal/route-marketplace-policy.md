# SmartRoad — Route Marketplace Policy

> **Last updated:** 2026-06-09  
> **A product of The Heedful**

---

## 1. Overview

The Route Marketplace allows users to share routes for free or sell them for profit. This policy governs all marketplace transactions.

## 2. Listing Routes

### 2.1 Eligibility
- You must be a registered user in good standing
- Routes must represent real, drivable/walkable paths
- You must have personal knowledge of the route

### 2.2 Prohibited Listings
- Routes that trespass on private property
- Routes that violate HOA or village access rules
- Routes that are dangerous or illegal
- Duplicate or fake routes
- Routes containing copyrighted or proprietary data

### 2.3 Listing Requirements
- Accurate description and distance
- Appropriate vehicle type classification
- Clear hazard and access notes
- Valid start and end points

## 3. Pricing

| Route Type | Price Range | Commission |
|------------|-------------|------------|
| Free route | ₱0 | 0% |
| Standard | ₱1 – ₱100 | 2% |
| Premium | ₱101 – ₱500 | 3% |
| Bundle (3+ routes) | Up to ₱1,000 | 2% |

## 4. Purchases

- All purchases are final
- Routes are licensed, not sold
- You may use purchased routes for personal navigation only
- Redistribution or resale is prohibited
- Refunds are at SmartRoad's discretion for technical issues only

## 5. Creator Payments

- Payouts are processed monthly
- Minimum payout threshold: ₱500
- Payment methods: GCash, bank transfer, or PayMaya
- SmartRoad deducts applicable taxes and fees

## 6. Quality Standards

Routes with consistently low ratings or reported inaccuracies may be:
- Removed from the marketplace
- Flagged for review
- Subject to creator reputation penalties

## 7. Dispute Resolution

Disputes between buyers and sellers should first be resolved directly. If unresolved, contact **marketplace@smartroad.app**.

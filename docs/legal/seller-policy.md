# SmartRoad — Seller Policy

> **Last updated:** 2026-06-09  
> **A product of The Heedful**

---

## 1. Seller Eligibility

To sell routes on SmartRoad, you must:
- Be a registered user with verified account
- Have a valid payment method for payouts
- Not have any active account restrictions
- Be at least 18 years old or have parental consent

## 2. Seller Responsibilities

- Ensure all listed routes are accurate and safe
- Respond to buyer questions within 48 hours
- Update route information when conditions change
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations
- Pay applicable taxes on earnings

## 3. Commission Structure

| Monthly Sales | Commission Rate |
|---------------|-----------------|
| ₱0 – ₱5,000 | 2% |
| ₱5,001 – ₱20,000 | 2.5% |
| ₱20,001+ | 3% |

## 4. Prohibited Conduct

- Manipulating ratings or reviews
- Listing fake or untested routes
- Plagiarizing other sellers' routes
- Soliciting off-platform payments
- Sharing buyer information

## 5. Account Suspension

Sellers who violate this policy may have their listings removed and accounts suspended. Repeated violations result in permanent ban from the marketplace.

## 6. Tax Compliance

Sellers are responsible for reporting earnings and paying applicable taxes. SmartRoad may issue tax forms where required by law.

# SmartRoad — HOA Partner Agreement

> **Last updated:** 2026-06-09  
> **A product of The Heedful**

---

## 1. Purpose

This agreement governs the partnership between SmartRoad and Homeowners' Associations (HOAs) for digital sticker management and gate access control.

## 2. HOA Responsibilities

- Provide accurate information about village boundaries and gates
- Define access rules (resident-only, public shortcut, etc.)
- Designate authorized personnel for sticker verification
- Maintain current geofence data for location-based passes
- Respond to access disputes in a timely manner

## 3. SmartRoad Responsibilities

- Maintain the digital sticker platform
- Process sticker verification requests
- Handle QR code generation for gate passes
- Provide access logs to HOA administrators
- Ensure data security and privacy

## 4. Revenue Sharing

| Service | SmartRoad Share | HOA Share |
|---------|-----------------|-----------|
| Day pass fees | 5% | 95% |
| Digital sticker sales | 3% | 97% |
| Physical sticker upload verification | ₱5 fee | 100% of sticker price |

## 5. Data Access

HOAs have access to:
- Access logs for their village
- Aggregate traffic data (anonymized)
- Sticker validation status

HOAs do NOT have access to:
- Personal user data beyond sticker info
- Location data of non-residents
- Payment details

## 6. Term and Termination

- Initial term: 12 months
- Auto-renewal unless 30 days notice given
- Either party may terminate for material breach
- Upon termination, active day passes remain valid

## 7. Contact

For HOA partnerships: **hoa@smartroad.app**
